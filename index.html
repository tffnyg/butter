day 03


Butter Noodles

food, recipe

ingredients
  • butter
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • garlic (crushed or sliced)
  • cheese (parmesan preffered)
  • pasta of your choice

step 1: Bring a pot of water to a boil.

step 2: Boil pasta until 1-2 mins under el dente, stir occasionally.

step 3: In a seperate pan with a lip at medium heat, add butter and garlic and cook until garlic is slightly browned.

step 4: Turn pan down to low and scoop/drain pasta into pan. Add a small spoonful of pasta water to the pan. (tip. do not add more pasta if when transfering the pasta to the pan water was added in the process.)

step 5: Add cheese on top of the pasta. Toss/Stir to combine. If pasta sauce is too watery add a little bit of milk or cornstarch to thicken the sauce.

step 6: Salt and Pepper to taste. Serve & Enjoy!
